* 🎯 **Scenario-based MCQs** — real situations, not definition lookups
* 🔀 **Answers shuffle** on every attempt
* 📊 Track **Answered, Correct & Wrong** live as you go
* 🧩 Covers every chapter in the **Foundations** track so far
* 🎨 Color-coded chapters, one accent per topic
* 🚀 Useful for **exam preparation and self-assessment**

> 💡 **Tip:** Try the quiz without checking your notes first. Use your results to identify weak areas and revisit those chapters before attempting it again.
