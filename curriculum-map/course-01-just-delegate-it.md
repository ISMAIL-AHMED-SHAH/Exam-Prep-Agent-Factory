## 🔄 The Delegation Loop

```text
┌──────────┐
│  DEFINE  │  Specify the result
└────┬─────┘
     ↓
┌──────────┐
│ DELEGATE │  Assign the job to AI
└────┬─────┘
     ↓
┌──────────┐
│ OBSERVE  │  Let AI execute
└────┬─────┘
     ↓
┌───────────┐
│ INTERVENE │  Correct or redirect
└────┬──────┘
     ↓
┌──────────┐
│  VERIFY  │  Check important claims
└────┬─────┘
     ↓
┌──────────┐
│  ACCEPT  │  Approve or send it back
└──────────┘
```

**Key principle:** AI performs the work; humans retain responsibility for verification and acceptance.
