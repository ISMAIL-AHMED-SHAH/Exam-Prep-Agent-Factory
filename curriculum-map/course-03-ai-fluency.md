## 🎯 The Four Core Competencies

| Competency | Key question | What to master |
|---|---|---|
| **Delegation** | Who should do what? | Define the problem, choose the right platform, divide the work |
| **Description** | What does AI need to know? | Specify the product, process, and performance |
| **Discernment** | Is the result trustworthy? | Evaluate the output, workflow, and independent AI behavior |
| **Diligence** | Is this responsible? | Protect data, be transparent, verify outcomes, retain accountability |

### 1. Delegation — Decide

Determine the goal before writing a prompt.

- Problem awareness: understand the objective, audience, success criteria, risks, and need for human judgment.
- Platform awareness: choose the appropriate model, search-enabled assistant, coding agent, or agent-capable system.
- Task delegation: decide what AI should do, what humans must do, and where collaboration is appropriate.

### 2. Description — Explain

Give AI enough information to perform the work well.

- **Product description:** define the desired output, audience, format, tone, length, and constraints.
- **Process description:** specify the steps, sequence, methods, examples, and checks.
- **Performance description:** define how AI should communicate, behave, challenge assumptions, and handle uncertainty.
- **Context engineering:** provide the documents, records, policies, examples, tools, and instructions needed for the task.

### 3. Discernment — Check

A polished response is not necessarily a correct response.

- **Product discernment:** check accuracy, completeness, consistency, evidence, and usefulness.
- **Process discernment:** determine whether the workflow saves time and improves results.
- **Performance discernment:** assess how an AI system behaves across many users and situations.
- Use evidence, source documents, explicit criteria, and human expertise to evaluate results.

### 4. Diligence — Own

Responsible AI use starts before the prompt and continues after the output.

- **Creation diligence:** use appropriate tools and protect personal or confidential data.
- **Transparency diligence:** disclose AI involvement when the context, rules, or impact require it.
- **Deployment diligence:** verify facts, calculations, permissions, fairness, and approval requirements.
- Human and organizational accountability remains essential.

## ⚙️ Three Ways to Work with AI

| Mode | Basic instruction | Human role | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Automation** | “Do this task.” | Define and review the task | Summarize a report |
| **Augmentation** | “Help me think.” | Collaborate and exercise judgment | Compare strategies |
| **Agency** | “Pursue this goal for me.” | Set boundaries and oversee outcomes | An agent manages a defined workflow |

> [!TIP]
> More AI autonomy does not automatically mean better results. Match the mode to the task, risks, permissions, and required human judgment.

## 🔄 The Practical 4D Operating Loop

```text
┌─────────────────────┐
│ 1. DELEGATE         │
│ Decide who does what│
└──────────┬──────────┘
           ▼
┌─────────────────────┐
│ 2. DESCRIBE         │
│ Provide context     │
└──────────┬──────────┘
           ▼
┌─────────────────────┐
│ 3. DISCERN          │
│ Inspect the result  │
└──────────┬──────────┘
           ▼
┌─────────────────────┐
│ 4. BE DILIGENT      │
│ Verify and own it   │
└──────────┬──────────┘
           │
           └──── Repeat when needed
```

The framework scales from an everyday chat to an AI-powered workflow or Digital FTE. At scale, these skills become specifications, reusable instructions, evaluation systems, governance controls, audit logs, and human-review processes.
